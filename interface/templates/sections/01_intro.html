What is Homomorphic Encryption?

Homomorphic Encryption (HE) lets you perform mathematical operations directly on encrypted data without ever decrypting it. The server computing the result never sees your real data — only unreadable ciphertext goes in, and an equally unreadable ciphertext comes out. Only the person holding the secret key can decrypt the final result.

This demo uses CKKS (via the TenSEAL library), a scheme designed for approximate arithmetic on real numbers — ideal for machine learning, where predictions are computed from weighted sums of numeric features.
